Account & Data Deletion

Package Name: com.useby.app

Users of the UseBy mobile application can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the UseBy App (Instant)

  1. Open the UseBy app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Akun (Account) tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the settings menu and tap Hapus Akun.
  4. Confirm by tapping Hapus Permanen.

Your authentication account and session data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app or wish to request manual deletion, send an email to 11231030@student.itk.ac.id with the subject "UseBy Account Deletion Request" and include the email address registered to your UseBy account. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and authentication credentials stored in Supabase Auth.
  • App Session & Product Data: Local product records and active session tokens on your device.

No personal data is retained after account deletion is completed. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.
