Privacy Policy

Application: UseBy
Package Name: com.useby.app
Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the UseBy mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"), created by the developer as a product management and expiry tracking service. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a personalized and reliable experience, the Application collects certain information when you register and use the service:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address and display name.
  • User-Generated Content: Information regarding the products you add, such as product names, categories, expiration dates, Period After Opening (PAO), and personal notes.
  • Network & Device Information: The Application requires network access (Internet and Network State) to sync your product data securely with our cloud backend.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide supporting infrastructure. Below are the third-party providers used:

  • Supabase: Used for authentication, backend database storage, and secure data synchronization. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: Used to render typography within the app interface. (Google Privacy Policy)

3. Data Storage and Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information. All communications between the Application and the backend servers are encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS). While we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your personal data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. User Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active. You have full rights to your data:

  • Delete Products: You can delete any individual product item at any time directly through the Application.
  • Delete Account: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly inside the app by opening the Akun (Account) screen and tapping Hapus Akun, or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page or contacting us via email. Upon confirmation, all your stored records in Supabase will be permanently removed.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
